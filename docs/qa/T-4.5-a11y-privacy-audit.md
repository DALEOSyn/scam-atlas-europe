# T-4.5 Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA) and privacy audit

- **Date:** 2026-11-20
- **Build:** `main` at b42783b (detached checkout), production build (`npm run build`) served by `vite preview` on 127.0.0.1:4394. The test harness (`vite.harness.config.ts`) ran on :4395 for the arc finale endings (C, A, B, R) and the interrupt and Fairmeasure fixtures.

- **Auditor:** qa-playtester

## Verdict: **FAIL**

Privacy (Red Line 7) **passes** on every check.

Accessibility fails on two criteria:
- **1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum):** two text elements fail contrast in the dark theme. The dark theme switches on automatically when the device is set to dark mode.
- **2.4.3 Focus Order:** pressing Escape in the HUD menu loses keyboard focus.

All three blockers below are small CSS or one-line fixes. Light mode, every device type, every size we tested and the keyboard flow are otherwise clean. See "What makes this a PASS" at the end.

## Blockers

**B1. Escape in the HUD menu drops keyboard focus to `<body>` (2.4.3 Focus Order), major.**
- Steps: start any scenario, Tab to Menu, press Enter, Tab to "Check mode" (or any menu item), press Escape.
- Expected: the menu closes and focus returns to the Menu button. Settings and Fairmeasure already do this (ScenarioView.tsx:527-537 and 548-555).
- Actual: the menu gets `hidden` while it holds focus, so `document.activeElement` is `<body>`. Checked after 800 ms. A screen reader loses its place. In Chromium the next Tab happens to resume at the device content, but other browsers may restart from the top of the page.
- Where: `src/ui/Hud.tsx:49-57`. The keydown handler is `if (e.key === 'Escape') toggle(false);` at line 53. It should also move focus to the Menu button (`[data-hud="menu"]`) when focus was inside `#hud-menu`.

**B2. "Translate" on a printed letter is 1.45:1 in dark mode (1.4.3 Contrast), blocker.**
- Steps: set the OS to dark mode, play Lisbon to the parking slip ("On the table: a letter").
- Actual: the `Translate` toggle text is #a9cbf7 on the letter paper #f4efe2 (axe: 1.45:1, needs 4.5:1). Screenshot: `dk-lisbon-1440-44.png`.
- Cause: `.translateToggle` uses `color: var(--color-link)` (`src/ui/devices/devices.module.css:252`). In dark mode that token is light blue, but the letter keeps a light paper background. `.letter .link` is already given `--letter-ink` (devices.module.css:1101-1103), but `.letter .translateToggle` (and `.letter .textButton`) is not.
- Fix: add `.letter .translateToggle, .letter .textButton { color: var(--letter-ink); }`, or another colour that passes AA on `--letter-paper`.

**B3. The "Gold" mark on the end screen is 2.19:1 in dark mode (1.4.3 Contrast), blocker.**
- Steps: set the OS to dark mode and finish Brussels with at least one habit card at Gold, so the end screen "Your Atlas" lists it. Reproduced in the real game and in harness arc B.
- Actual: `.goldMark` text is #6f5200 on #1e2130 (2.19:1). Screenshot: `dk-brussels-1440-64.png` (the mark is further down the page).
- Cause: `src/ui/Scene.module.css:506-510` uses `--color-stamp-gold`, which is defined only for light (`src/styles/tokens.css:141`). The dark block (`tokens.css:172` onward) does not override any `--color-stamp-*` token.
- Fix: add dark values for `--color-stamp-gold`, `--color-stamp-silver` and `--color-stamp-bronze` that reach at least 4.5:1 on `--color-surface` and `--color-bg` in dark. Then check every stamp use (stamps, chapter done, end screen) in dark mode.

## Minors (6)

- **m1. Forced colours: filled primary buttons lose their outline.** "Start", "Continue" and "Back to the chapters" read as plain text, because the fill is removed and the border is `0`. This is not a WCAG SC failure but it is a Windows High Contrast usability gap. Where: `src/ui/ChapterList.module.css:63`, `src/ui/DebriefView.module.css:91`, `src/ui/ScenarioView.module.css:229`, `src/ui/Fairmeasure.module.css:149`, `src/app.module.css:93` (and the same pattern in `Scene.module.css:66`). Fix: use `border: 2px solid transparent`, or add an `@media (forced-colors: active)` border. Screenshots: `fc-intro.png`, `fc-debrief.png`.
- **m2. The HUD menu stays open when focus Tabs out of it.** At 375 px it partly covers the device header: the TRUNDLE sender button had 1 of 3 sample points visible. Not entirely hidden, so 2.4.11 still passes. Close the menu on `focusout` that leaves `menuWrap`. Where: `src/ui/Hud.tsx:49-57`. Screenshot: `menu-tabout-375.png`.
- **m3. No h1 on scenario screens.** The top heading is the h2 device heading ("Text message from TRUNDLE"), and the "What happened" and "Limit the damage" steps use h3. Screen-reader users jumping to h1 find nothing. This is axe best practice `page-has-heading-one`, not a WCAG tag. Where: `src/ui/ScenarioView.tsx` (heading levels).
- **m4. `document.title` never changes** (no `document.title` anywhere in `src/`). 2.4.2 passes, but per-screen titles ("Berlin · Message 3 of 10 · Scam Atlas: Europe") would help screen-reader and tab-switching users.
- **m5. Leaving the help screen does not return focus to the help button.** "Back to the story" puts focus on the current panel (`li[tabindex=-1]`, "Panel 1 of 4…") rather than "Where to get help". This is reasonable, but it is inconsistent with Settings, Fairmeasure and the Check card, which return to their trigger. Where: `src/ui/ScenePlayer.tsx:276, 282`.
- **m6. The idle "Jump to your options" button still takes space.** At 320 px it leaves a blank band of about 56 px under the HUD. At 400 % zoom (320×256) the sticky HUD plus the jump button use 112 of 256 px. Nothing was obscured, but reading space is tight. Where: `src/ui/ScenarioView.tsx:781` (`jumpIdle`) and `ScenarioView.module.css` `.jump`. Screenshot: `r320-stockholm-320-46.png`.

### New findings

**R1. On phones the idle jump bar collapse moves the content by about 60 px when the bar comes back (minor, related to WCAG 2.2.2 and 3.2 predictability, but not a failure).**
- Steps: 375×700 (or 360×740), Paris message 1, timers off. Scroll down about 70 px until the options come into view. The bar hides and the content stays still. Then scroll up by 12–24 px.
- Expected: the content moves by the scroll amount. CR-27 says: "its space stays reserved … (no layout jump when it hides)".
- Actual: the bar comes back in the flow at scroll offset 0. Scroll anchoring cannot make up for it at the top of the page, so the email jumps down 60 px after a 12 px scroll. The device heading moves from 56 to 128 px. Scrolling down near the top does the same in reverse whenever the options come into view at a scroll offset under 60 px. Further down the page, Chromium's scroll anchoring hides the shift. With `overflow-anchor: none`, which stands in for a browser without scroll anchoring, the 60 px jump happens at every hide and show (320×640 Berlin: the content moved 84 px for a 24 px scroll). WebKit was not available in this environment to check iOS Safari directly.
- Screenshots: `m6-jump-B-scrolled.png`, then `m6-jump-C-back.png`.
- Where: `src/ui/ScenarioView.module.css:392-396` (`.jumpIdle { display: none; }` under `max-width: 599px`).
- Fix: keep the reserved space (`visibility: hidden`) and make the bar itself shorter on phones, so the band is small but nothing moves.

**R2. Escape pressed within about 15 ms of opening the HUD menu is lost (minor, 2.1.1 / 2.4.3 edge case).**
- Steps: by script, with the CPU busy. Enter on Menu, Tab, Tab, then Escape about 5–15 ms after the Enter.
- Actual: the menu stays open with focus on its item. This happened 2 of 15 times at 320 and 2 of 15 at 1440. With 120 ms or more between keys: 0 of 15 at 320, and 8 of 8 at every size.
- Cause: the Escape listener is added in a passive `useEffect` (`src/ui/Hud.tsx:50`), which Preact runs only after the next paint. A person cannot normally press keys that fast, but a busy CI runner or scripted assistive tech can.
- Fix: register the listener in `useLayoutEffect`, or handle `onKeyDown` on `menuWrap`.

**R3. Heading levels skip from h1 to h3 on the consequence and damage-control steps (advisory, axe best practice `heading-order`, not WCAG-tagged).**
- On the "What happened" step the headings are h1 "Berlin · Message 1 of 10" and then h3 "What happened", because the device h2 is gone at that point.
- Where: `src/ui/ScenarioView.tsx:985` (`consequence-h`), and 170, 232 and 266 (damage headings).
- Fix: make these h2, or keep an h2 above them.
