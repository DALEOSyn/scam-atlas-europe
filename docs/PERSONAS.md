# Playtest personas (used by qa-playtester and game-designer)

1. **Lena, 16, Vienna.** Plays on a phone between classes. Lives in messengers and marketplaces. Low patience for text.
2. **Marco, 38, Milan.** Accounts assistant, busy, processes invoices and supplier emails. Target for CEO fraud and invoice redirection.
3. **Ingrid, 71, Gothenburg.** Uses online banking and an eID app, careful but trusting of authority. Target for fake bank/police calls.
4. **Tomasz, 27, Kraków.** Sells second-hand items online, crypto-curious. Target for marketplace and investment scams.
5. **Aoife, 45, Cork.** Uses a screen reader. Plays with keyboard only.
6. **Yusuf, 30, Brussels.** New arrival, second-language English/French. Target for rental, job and official-letter scams.
