# Release notes

## 1.0.0 (release candidate, not yet published)

This is the first public release. Publishing waits for Gate G4 in `docs/DECISIONS.md`.

### The game
- **11 chapters and 108 scenarios.** Home (tutorial), Berlin, Amsterdam, Paris, Madrid, Warsaw, Rome, Lisbon, Dublin, Stockholm and the Brussels finale.
- **Covered threats** include: phishing email, smishing, messenger and "new number" scams, spoofed bank and police calls, CEO and invoice fraud, fake shops and rentals, malicious QR codes, account takeover, one-time-code and e-ID abuse, remote-access and tech-support scams, investment and recovery scams, and money-mule offers.
- **44 illustrated story scenes.** Alex's investment-scam story runs across the chapters and has five endings, chosen by what the player actually did.
- **Debriefs.** Every scenario ends with one covering the warning signs, the check that would have worked, and what to do if you already fell for it.
- **Stamps.** Bronze, silver and gold, with a "How stamps work" explanation before each chapter.
- **Help screens.** Checked reporting, bank, support and crisis contacts for 10 countries, plus an EU / other-country default with 112.

### Accessibility and privacy
- Audited against WCAG 2.2 AA (`docs/qa/T-4.5-a11y-privacy-audit.md`).
- Keyboard and screen-reader play; Extended or no timers; reduced motion; dark mode.
- No accounts, tracking or analytics. Progress stays in the browser, and the game plays offline once loaded.

### Known limits
- English only. Further languages are decided at G5.
- Help contacts were checked in November 2026. They are re-checked within 30 days before release.
