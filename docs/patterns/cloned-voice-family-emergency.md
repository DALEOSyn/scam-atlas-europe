# Pattern brief: `cloned-voice-family-emergency`

**Chapter:** 6 Rome (GDD §18: Davide (19) pays after a voice message that sounds like his sister, from a new number; the family code word with Teo) · **Status:** draft for review (T-3.8r) · **Researched:** 2026-11-19 · **Owner:** fraud-researcher

Builds on `messenger-family-impersonation.md` (Amsterdam): variant A, the "new number", and variant C, the cloned voice. This brief adds Italian and EU sources for the voice part, and it rules on the **voice message** question.

## 1. Name and aliases
- **English:** voice-cloning scam; AI voice scam; deepfake voice of a relative; family-emergency scam.
- **Local:** *clonazione vocale* (the ESAs' Italian edition) [eu-esas-ai-scams-factsheet-it]; *voice cloning* (NL government platform) [nl-veiliginternetten-voice-cloning].
- **Game label:** a Kettle message from a new number, with an audio clip in the sister's voice. Kettle and Larchwick are fictional brands.
- **Where it is documented:**
  - **EU:** the ESAs warn about people pretending to be a relative, about "clonazione vocale", and about fake "audio che imitano la voce"; they advise a family "parola sicura" [eu-esas-ai-scams-factsheet-it], [eu-esas-ai-scams-factsheet-nl]. The EPC lists cloned voices in family-emergency scams [eu-epc-threats-2025].
  - **Italy:** a ministry brochure says voices can be recorded from short answers or silent calls and "manipulated with voice cloning techniques". It cites a February 2025 Italian case where a government minister's voice was cloned to ask for money for false emergencies [it-mur-smishing-vishing].
  - A Rome case in May 2026 reportedly used a husband's cloned voice. That is a news-agency claim, not official [it-open-roma-finti-poliziotti-2026] (news, supporting only).
  - **NL:** official and banking pages warn of AI-imitated voices of relatives [nl-veiliginternetten-voice-cloning], [nl-fraudehelpdesk-hulpvraag-campaign], [nl-veiligbankieren-voice-cloning-2023].
  - **FR:** an official case of a deepfake video call imitating a chief executive's face and voice [fr-dgsi-flash-117-2025]. That is the executive variant, not a relative.
- **What the sources do not say:**
  - No European official source gives a prevalence figure.
  - Do not say "this is common" or "this is rising" on screen.
  - The evidence is that it **exists and is warned about**.

## 2. Channel and psychological levers
**Channel:** a messenger message from an **unknown number** ("it's me, new phone"), followed by the money request [nl-fraudehelpdesk-hulpvraag-campaign], [nl-politie-hulpvraagfraude-2025]. In the voice variant, the "relative" is heard in a call or in audio [nl-veiliginternetten-voice-cloning], [eu-esas-ai-scams-factsheet-it].

**Voice message (GDD wording) — ruling:**
- The ESAs (IT edition) and the Dutch government platform speak of fake **audio** that imitates someone's voice. They don't limit it to live calls [eu-esas-ai-scams-factsheet-it], [nl-veiliginternetten-voice-cloning].
- No official source opened this session (IT, NL, DE, EU) describes a cloned **voice note inside a messenger chat** specifically. The Dutch sources describe calls [nl-fraudehelpdesk-hulpvraag-campaign], [nl-veiligbankieren-voice-cloning-2023]. Italian police pages could not be opened.
- **Verdict:** a short audio clip in the chat is acceptable as "fake audio", under three conditions:
  1. The debrief says "a voice can be faked", not "scammers send voice notes".
  2. The scene puts the tell on the **new number + money + no call**, not on the audio's quality.
  3. No claim is made about how common voice notes are.
- **Safer alternative:** a short call from the new number in the sister's voice, which the Dutch sources describe directly.

**The request:** urgent money for a crisis (lost phone, a bill, an accident), often to **someone else's account** or a payment link, often with a second request after [nl-politie-hulpvraagfraude-2025], [nl-veiliginternetten-hulpvraag].

**Levers:** love and fear for a sibling; a voice you know ("if you hear a familiar voice, you trust it faster") [nl-veiliginternetten-voice-cloning]; urgency; an excuse why they can't talk normally [nl-veiliginternetten-hulpvraag].

## 3. Red flags a normal person can spot
1. **A relative writes from a new number** and asks for money [nl-fraudehelpdesk-hulpvraag-campaign], [eu-esas-ai-scams-factsheet-it].
2. **A voice that sounds right proves nothing.** Audio can be made to imitate a voice from a short recording [eu-esas-ai-scams-factsheet-it], [nl-veiliginternetten-voice-cloning], [it-mur-smishing-vishing].
3. **Urgency**: today, right now [nl-fraudehelpdesk-hulpvraag-campaign].
4. **No normal call**: an excuse why they can't pick up on their usual number [nl-veiliginternetten-hulpvraag].
5. **Pay someone else's account**, or a link, rather than the person's own account [nl-veiliginternetten-hulpvraag], [nl-politie-hulpvraagfraude-2025].
   - In-game cue: Italy is in the euro area, so the Larchwick payee-name check applies. "Doesn't match" against the sister's name is a strong warning. A "match" with a stranger's name proves only whose account it is [eu-ec-ipr-vop-2025], [eu-ecb-ipr] (see `messenger-family-impersonation.md` §3.5).
6. **A second request** after the first payment [nl-politie-hulpvraagfraude-2025].

*Not to teach as reliable tells:* a metallic sound, odd stresses or delays. BSI listed these in 2022, but voice quality improves [de-bsi-deepfakes]. Spelling and tone aren't reliable either, because AI removes errors [eu-esas-ai-scams-factsheet-nl].

## 4. The verification habit
**"Before you pay, call them on the number you already have saved. A voice can be copied; your saved number can't be. Agree a family code word for money requests."** [nl-fraudehelpdesk-hulpvraag-campaign], [nl-veiliginternetten-voice-cloning], [eu-esas-ai-scams-factsheet-it]

- If you can't reach them, call another family member [nl-fraudehelpdesk-hulpvraag-campaign], [nl-veiligbankieren-hulpvraag].
- **The code word is an extra, not the main check.** It can be forgotten under stress, and scammers can say "I forgot". The call-back to the saved number is the core habit [nl-veiligbankieren-voice-cloning-2023].
- **Prevention for debriefs:**
  - agree how you'd really ask for help;
  - keep the code word out of chats and social media;
  - share fewer voice clips publicly.

  Sources: [nl-fraudehelpdesk-hulpvraag-campaign], [nl-veiliginternetten-voice-cloning], [it-mur-smishing-vishing].
  - The "voice clips" point is an inference from "a short recording" plus "share less online". It is not a quoted rule.

## 5. Already caught? (generic + Italy where verified 2026-11-19)
1. **Call your bank now** through its official channels. Ask it to stop or recall the transfer [it-bdi-truffe-cyber], [eu-esas-ai-scams-factsheet-it].
2. **Don't pay the second request.** Stop replying [nl-politie-hulpvraagfraude-2025].
3. **Keep the evidence**: the chat, the audio, the new number and the payment details [nl-politie-aangifte-hulpvraagfraude].
4. **Tell the real person** on their saved number, so they can warn others.
5. **Report to the police.** In Italy, the Polizia Postale e delle comunicazioni (per the Banca d'Italia) [it-bdi-truffe-cyber]. Report the number in the messenger too. In NL the police say to do that last, because the chat can disappear [nl-politie-aangifte-hulpvraagfraude]. That timing was not checked for Italy.
6. **Refunds:** not promised. Italian rules for authorised-but-deceived payments were not checked.
7. **Tone line:** "This works on people who care, at any age. Calling the bank fast was the right first move."

## 6. Variations by country
- **IT:** EU guidance in Italian [eu-esas-ai-scams-factsheet-it]; the minister voice-clone case [it-mur-smishing-vishing]; fake-relative calls about accidents (offline pick-up out of scope) [it-carabinieri-contro-truffe].
- **NL:** "new number" chats, payment-request links, cloned voices in calls [nl-fraudehelpdesk-hulpvraag-campaign], [nl-veiliginternetten-voice-cloning].
- **FR:** deepfake video and voice of an executive (workplace) [fr-dgsi-flash-117-2025].

## 7. Genuine-twin notes
- A real "new number" message with **no money request**, which the player can confirm by calling the old number (`messenger-family-impersonation.md` §7).
- A real voice message from the sister on her **saved** contact about something ordinary. Familiar voices are normal; the tell is never the voice alone.

## 8. Game-design notes (non-binding)
- **Davide (19) falls:** shown as capable and fond of his sister. The lever is love and urgency (Red Line 5). Bruna and the player guide damage control: bank first, then the sister on her saved number, then evidence and a report.
- **Family code word:** Teo and the player agree one. The debrief adds "if you can't remember it, hang up and call the saved number anyway".
- **Audio:** illustration and transcript, no synthetic audio, even fictional (as decided for Paris, GDD §23.12 item 7).

## 10. Confidence
**High** for the "new number + money" pattern, the red flags and the call-back habit. **Medium** for the cloned-voice element: the EU, an Italian ministry and Dutch official sources warn about it, and there is one official Italian case (a minister's voice), but there are no prevalence figures. **Low–Medium** for the voice note specifically (see §2 ruling).

**Unverified / caveats:**
- Cloned voice notes inside a messenger chat: no official source.
- Italian police pages could not be opened (access denied). No Italian police text on voice cloning was read.
- No figures. Don't put a number on screen.
